Fly the Bay Area as a bird in 3D

Type any Bay Area address and spawn as a bird above it. The world below is real: the actual street grid, real building footprints at their real heights, the parks, the freeways, the Bay, the hills — streamed live from OpenStreetMap and rendered as a warm golden-hour dreamworld in your browser. No account, no install, no API key.
